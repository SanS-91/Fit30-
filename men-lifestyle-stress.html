STRESS MANAGEMENT

Quản Lý Stress

Meditation · Breathing · Cold exposure · Journaling

Chronic stress phá hủy testosterone, tăng cortisol, tích belly fat, và giảm sleep quality. Không phải "relax more" - cần specific techniques và daily practice.

Tại Sao Stress Quan Trọng?

Cortisol impact lên 4 areas chính

-30%

Testosterone

Chronic stress giảm testosterone 30%. Cortisol và testosterone có inverse relationship. High cortisol = low T.

+50%

Muscle Breakdown

Cortisol là catabolic hormone - phá hủy muscle tissue. Chronic stress = khó build muscle, dễ mất cơ.

+40%

Belly Fat Storage

Cortisol tăng visceral fat storage (belly fat). Stress eating + insulin resistance = double impact.

-25%

Sleep Quality

Stress tăng alertness, giảm deep sleep. Vicious cycle: poor sleep → more stress → worse sleep.

Key insight: Stress không phải enemy - acute stress (workout, cold exposure) là good. Problem là chronic stress - không có recovery. Need balance.

5 Techniques Giảm Stress

Evidence-based, practical, daily applicable

10 min/day

Meditation

8 weeks meditation giảm cortisol 25%, tăng gray matter density. Start small, build consistency.

  • Ngồi thoải mái, lưng thẳng
  • Focus vào breath (in/out)
  • Khi mind wander, gently return
  • Start 5 min, build lên 10-20 min
  • Apps: Headspace, Waking Up, Insight Timer
5 min

Breathing Exercises

Breathing là remote control cho nervous system. Slow breathing activate parasympathetic (rest & digest).

  • 4-7-8: Inhale 4s, hold 7s, exhale 8s
  • Box breathing: 4-4-4-4 equal parts
  • Physiological sigh: 2 inhales + long exhale
  • 5-10 cycles, notice calm
10 min/day

Journaling

Writing về stress giảm anxiety 30%. Externalize thoughts = giảm mental load. Brain dump technique.

  • Viết 3 things stressed về
  • Viết 3 things grateful cho
  • Brain dump: viết mọi thứ trong đầu
  • Reflect: what can I control?
  • Evening journaling tốt hơn morning
2-5 min

Cold Exposure

Cold shower tăng dopamine 250%, giảm cortisol, build resilience. Stress inoculation technique.

  • Start: 30s cold cuối shower
  • Build lên 2-5 minutes
  • Focus vào breath (đừng hold)
  • Temperature: 10-15°C là đủ
  • Không cần ice bath - cold shower OK
20-30 min

Nature Exposure

20 phút trong nature giảm cortisol 21%. Forest bathing, park walk, garden time. Green space = mental health.

  • Walk trong park/green space 20-30 phút
  • No phone, no music - just observe
  • Notice: trees, birds, sky, sounds
  • Forest bathing (Shinrin-yoku): slow, mindful walk
  • Even 10 min window view có effect
  • Combine với walking meditation

Breathing Patterns

4-7-8 Breathing
4 → 7 → 8
Best for: Sleep, anxiety, acute stress
How: Inhale 4s → Hold 7s → Exhale 8s
Why: Long exhale activate parasympathetic nervous system
Box Breathing
4 → 4 → 4 → 4
Best for: Focus, performance, Navy SEALs use
How: Inhale 4s → Hold 4s → Exhale 4s → Hold 4s
Why: Equal parts = balance, control, presence

Stress Management Tips

3 strategies cho work-life balance

Work-Life Balance

Boundaries không phải selfish - là sustainable. Work will expand to fill time available. Set limits.

  • Set work hours và stick to them
  • No work email sau 19:00
  • Separate workspace (không work từ bed)
  • Weekly review: am I balanced?
  • Protect weekends cho recovery

Learning to Say No

Mỗi "yes" là "no" với cái khác. Say no để protect time, energy, priorities. Not rude - necessary.

  • "Let me check and get back to you"
  • "I don't have capacity right now"
  • "That doesn't align with my priorities"
  • No JADE: don't Justify, Argue, Defend, Explain
  • Practice: start with small nos

Delegation & Systems

Not everything needs your attention. Delegate, automate, eliminate. Focus on high-impact only.

  • List tasks: do, delegate, delete
  • Automate: bills, emails, reminders
  • Outsource: cleaning, grocery delivery
  • Say no to low-impact commitments
  • Focus on 20% tasks that give 80% results
💡
Stress equation: Stress = Demand / Resources. Increase resources (sleep, nutrition, support) hoặc decrease demands (say no, delegate). Both work.

Bắt đầu quản lý stress

Pick 1-2 techniques từ guide này. Practice daily 2 tuần. Track progress. Small consistent actions > occasional big efforts.
