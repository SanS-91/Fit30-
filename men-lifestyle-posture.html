Tư Thế Văn Phòng

Ergonomic setup · Break schedule · Desk exercises

8 tiếng ngồi sai tư thế = 8 tiếng phá hủy cơ thể. Guide này giúp bạn setup workstation đúng cách, giảm pain, và maintain posture tốt dù ngồi nhiều.

90-90-90 Rule

Golden rule cho sitting posture

Tư thế ngồi chuẩn: 3 góc 90 độ - ankles, knees, hips. Giữ spine neutral, shoulders relaxed, screen ngang tầm mắt. Đây là foundation - mọi ergonomic setup khác đều build trên rule này.

90°
Ankles
Bàn chân phẳng trên sàn hoặc footrest
90°
Knees
Gối ngang hoặc thấp hơn hips một chút
90°
Hips
Lưng tựa vào chair, spine neutral
💡
Quick check: Nếu feet không chạm sàn → dùng footrest. Nếu knees cao hơn hips → hạ chair hoặc dùng cushion. Nếu phải nhìn xuống screen → raise monitor.

Ergonomic Setup

4 components cần optimize

Laptop Height & Stand

Laptop quá thấp = neck flexion = neck pain, headaches. Top of screen phải ngang tầm mắt.

  • Dùng laptop stand raise screen lên
  • Top of screen ngang eye level
  • Distance: 50-70cm từ mắt
  • External keyboard + mouse khi dùng stand
  • Angle: 10-20° tilt back
Recommendation: Laptop stand $20-40 + external keyboard/mouse. ROI: giảm neck pain 80%.

Mouse Selection

Traditional mouse = forearm pronation = wrist strain. Vertical mouse giữ forearm neutral position.

  • Vertical mouse: giảm wrist strain 50%
  • Trackball mouse: alternative tốt
  • Size: vừa với hand, không quá lớn
  • Wireless để giảm cable clutter
  • Switch hands nếu có thể
Recommendation: Logitech MX Vertical hoặc Anker Vertical. Budget option: Jelly Comb vertical mouse.

Keyboard Setup

Keyboard quá cao = shoulder elevation = tension. Ergonomic keyboard giảm wrist extension.

  • Keyboard ngang elbow height
  • Split keyboard cho wrist neutral
  • Tent angle: 5-10° (không quá cao)
  • Wrist rest: chỉ dùng khi typing pause
  • Mechanical keyboard: tactile feedback tốt hơn
Recommendation: Logitech ERGO K860 (split) hoặc Keychron K8 (mechanical, budget).

Monitor Distance & Height

Monitor quá gần = eye strain. Quá xa = lean forward = back pain. Height sai = neck pain.

  • Distance: 50-70cm (arm's length)
  • Height: top of screen ngang eye level
  • Tilt: 10-20° back
  • Dual monitors: angle 30° inward
  • Primary screen thẳng trước mặt
Recommendation: Monitor arm mount (flexible positioning). 24-27" screen cho desk work.

Break Schedule

2 rules để giảm strain

20-20-20

Eye Strain Prevention

Mỗi 20 phút, nhìn vật cách 20 feet (6m) trong 20 giây. Giảm digital eye strain, headaches, dry eyes.

  1. Set timer mỗi 20 phút
  2. Nhìn ra xa (cửa sổ, wall far away)
  3. Focus 20 giây, blink nhiều
  4. Return to work
60/5

Hourly Movement Break

Mỗi 60 phút ngồi, đứng lên 5 phút. Walk, stretch, refill water. Prevents stiffness, DVT, back pain.

  1. Stand up khỏi chair
  2. Walk 2-3 phút (refill water, bathroom)
  3. 2 phút stretching (neck, shoulders, hips)
  4. Sit back down với posture check
⚠️
Reality check: Perfect posture không tồn tại. Best posture = next posture. Movement > static position. Change positions thường xuyên.

Standing Desk Guide

When & how to use đúng cách

Standing desk không phải solution cho mọi vấn đề. Standing quá lâu = leg pain, varicose veins. Key là alternating giữa sitting và standing.

Standing Desk Height

  • Elbows 90° khi typing
  • Screen ngang eye level
  • Weight distribute đều 2 chân
  • Anti-fatigue mat nếu đứng lâu

When to Stand

  • Sau 45-60 phút sitting
  • During calls/meetings
  • Reading emails (light tasks)
  • Không đứng khi deep work
💡
Budget option: Laptop riser + external keyboard/mouse. Không cần $500 standing desk. Start simple, upgrade sau.

5-Minute Desk Exercises

4 areas cần mobilize

2 min

Neck

Giảm neck tension, headaches từ screen time.

  • Chin tucks: 10 reps
  • Neck rotations: 5 mỗi bên
  • Ear to shoulder: 30s mỗi bên
  • Chin to chest: 30s hold
1 min

Shoulders

Counteract rounded shoulders từ typing.

  • Shoulder rolls: 10 forward, 10 back
  • Arm circles: 10 mỗi direction
  • Chest stretch (doorway): 30s
  • Scapular squeezes: 10 reps
1 min

Back

Reduce lower back stiffness từ sitting.

  • Seated cat-cow: 10 reps
  • Seated spinal twist: 30s mỗi bên
  • Standing back extension: 10 reps
  • Child's pose (nếu có space): 30s
1 min

Hips

Open tight hip flexors từ sitting.

  • Seated figure-4: 30s mỗi bên
  • Standing hip flexor stretch: 30s
  • Glute squeezes: 10 reps
  • Standing knee hugs: 5 mỗi bên
💡
When to do: Sau mỗi hourly break (60/5 rule). Total: 5 phút mỗi giờ = 40 phút/ngày. Preventive > reactive.

Common Office Pain Solutions

3 pain points phổ biến + fixes

Lower Back

Lower Back Pain

Cause: Prolonged sitting, weak core, tight hip flexors, poor lumbar support.
  • Lumbar roll hoặc cushion cho chair
  • Hip flexor stretches mỗi giờ
  • Core strengthening (plank, bird dog)
  • Standing desk alternating
  • Dead hangs cho spinal decompression
Neck & Shoulders

Neck Pain & Tension

Cause: Screen quá thấp, forward head posture, stress, phone use.
  • Raise monitor đến eye level
  • Chin tucks exercises daily
  • Heat therapy (heating pad 15min)
  • Massage ball cho trigger points
  • Reduce phone use (text neck)
Wrists & Hands

Wrist Strain & Carpal Tunnel

Cause: Repetitive typing, wrist extension, poor mouse position.
  • Vertical mouse hoặc trackball
  • Wrist stretches mỗi giờ
  • Keyboard tray để wrists neutral
  • Take frequent breaks (20-20-20)
  • Night wrist splints nếu severe
Red flags: Numbness, tingling, weakness, pain radiating down arms/legs → consult doctor/physio. Đừng ignore nerve symptoms.
