10 Daily Habits

Foundation cho lifestyle cân bằng

1
Morning

Morning Sunlight (5-10 min)

Ánh sáng mặt trời buổi sáng điều chỉnh circadian rhythm, tăng cortisol tự nhiên, cải thiện sleep quality.

  • Regulates sleep-wake cycle
  • Boosts morning energy
  • Improves mood (serotonin)
  • Free vitamin D
2
Breakfast

Protein Breakfast

30-40g protein trong bữa sáng giảm cravings, tăng satiety, preserve muscle mass.

  • Reduces hunger throughout day
  • Stabilizes blood sugar
  • Preserves muscle mass
  • Boosts metabolism
3
Throughout day

8K-10K Steps

Walking là underrated exercise. Burns calories, improves cardiovascular health, clears mind.

  • Burns 300-500 kcal/day
  • Improves cardiovascular health
  • Reduces stress
  • Easy to track (phone/watch)
4
Throughout day

3-4L Water

Hydration ảnh hưởng energy, focus, performance. Đa số người uống không đủ nước.

  • Improves cognitive function
  • Boosts physical performance
  • Supports recovery
  • Reduces headaches
5
10 min

10-Min Stretching

Daily stretching maintains mobility, reduces injury risk, counteracts sitting.

  • Maintains joint mobility
  • Reduces muscle tension
  • Prevents injuries
  • Improves posture
6
10 min

10-Min Meditation/Breathing

Mental health = physical health. Meditation reduces cortisol, improves focus, emotional regulation.

  • Reduces stress & anxiety
  • Improves focus & clarity
  • Better emotional regulation
  • Lowers blood pressure
7
Evening

No Phone 1h Before Bed

Blue light suppress melatonin 50%. Social media increases anxiety. Protect sleep quality.

  • Improves sleep quality
  • Reduces anxiety
  • Better relationships
  • More mindful evenings
8
23:00

Sleep by 23h

Consistent bedtime regulates circadian rhythm. 7-8h sleep = testosterone, recovery, cognitive function.

  • Regulates circadian rhythm
  • Maximizes deep sleep
  • Boosts testosterone
  • Improves recovery
9
Evening

Gratitude Journaling

Writing 3 things grateful for rewires brain for positivity. Reduces stress, improves relationships.

  • Increases happiness
  • Reduces stress
  • Improves relationships
  • Better sleep quality
10
Evening

Prepare Tomorrow

Lay out outfit, prep meals, review schedule. Reduces morning decision fatigue.

  • Reduces morning stress
  • Saves time
  • Better meal choices
  • More productive mornings

Habit Stacking Guide

How to build habits that stick

Habit stacking = attaching new habits to existing ones. Formula: "After [CURRENT HABIT], I will [NEW HABIT]." Leverages existing neural pathways.

After [CURRENT], I will [NEW]
Attach new habits to existing routines

Morning Routine Stack

After I pour my morning coffee, I will drink 500ml water.

After I brush my teeth, I will do 10 min stretching.

Work Routine Stack

After I sit at my desk, I will do 2 min breathing exercises.

After lunch, I will walk 10 minutes.

Evening Routine Stack

After dinner, I will write 3 things I'm grateful for.

After I turn off my phone, I will read 10 pages.

Bedtime Routine Stack

After I get into bed, I will do 5 min meditation.

After I set my alarm, I will prepare tomorrow's outfit.

💡
Key principle: Make it obvious, attractive, easy, satisfying. Environment design > willpower.

Tips for Success

3 principles for lasting change

Start Small

Don't try to change everything at once. Pick 1-2 habits, master them, then add more.

  • 2-minute rule: make it so easy you can't say no
  • Focus on consistency, not perfection
  • Celebrate small wins
  • Don't break the chain twice

Consistency > Intensity

10 min daily > 2h weekly. Build the habit first, optimize later.

  • Same time, same place daily
  • Track progress visually
  • Accountability partner helps
  • Forgive slip-ups, keep going

Track Progress

What gets measured gets managed. Use the tracker above or any method that works.

  • Visual tracking (calendar, app)
  • Weekly reviews
  • Adjust based on data
  • Focus on trends, not daily perfection
Remember: 21 days to build a habit, 90 days to make it lifestyle. Be patient. Trust the process.
