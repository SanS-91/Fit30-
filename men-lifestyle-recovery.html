RECOVERY PROTOCOLS

Phục Hồi Thông Minh

Foam rolling · Sauna · Ice bath · Contrast therapy

Recovery không phải lazy - nó là khi bạn actually grow. Training breaks you down, recovery builds you up. Optimize cả hai.

Foam Rolling

Self-myofascial release cho 8 muscle groups

2-3 min

Calves

Giảm tightness từ running, jumping. Prevents Achilles issues.

  • Ngồi, đặt foam roller dưới calves
  • Nâng hips, roll từ ankle đến knee
  • Tìm tender spots, hold 30s
  • Rotate leg để hit不同角度
2-3 min

Quads

Tight quads từ squatting, sitting. Pulls pelvis forward.

  • Plank position, roller dưới quads
  • Roll từ hip đến knee
  • Leg hơi rotate để hit VMO
  • Hold tender spots 30-45s
2-3 min

IT Band

Common issue cho runners. Lateral knee pain.

  • Side-lying, roller dưới outer thigh
  • Roll từ hip đến knee
  • Đừng roll trực tiếp trên knee
  • Very tender - go slow
2-3 min

Glutes

Tight glutes từ sitting. Causes lower back pain.

  • Ngồi trên roller, cross ankle over knee
  • Lean về phía crossed leg
  • Roll circular motions
  • Find trigger points, hold 30s
2-3 min

Hamstrings

Tight hams từ sitting, deadlifts. Limits hip mobility.

  • Ngồi, roller dưới hamstrings
  • Hands behind để lift hips
  • Roll từ knee đến glutes
  • Rotate leg để hit不同角度
2-3 min

Upper Back

Counteract rounded shoulders từ desk work.

  • Ngửa, roller dưới upper back
  • Hands behind head
  • Roll từ mid-back đến shoulders
  • Don't roll lower back
1-2 min

Lats

Tight lats từ pull-ups, rows. Limits overhead mobility.

  • Side-lying, roller dưới armpit area
  • Arm extended overhead
  • Roll slowly down lat
  • Very tender - breathe through it
1-2 min

Chest

Open up chest từ bench press, desk posture.

  • Face-down, roller dưới chest
  • Arm extended to side
  • Roll slowly across pec
  • Hold tender spots 20-30s
💡
Rules: Roll slow (1 inch/second). Breathe deeply. 7/10 discomfort max - không nên đau đớn. Best post-workout hoặc trước bed.

Massage

Self-massage vs Professional

DIY

Self-Massage

Daily maintenance, cost-effective, convenient. Dùng tools để replicate professional techniques.

  • Tools: Foam roller, lacrosse ball, massage gun
  • Frequency: Daily 10-15 phút
  • Best for: Maintenance, tight spots
  • Techniques: Rolling, trigger point, percussion
  • Cost: $20-200 one-time (tools)
Recommended: Foam roller + lacrosse ball + massage gun (Theragun/Hypervolt). Total ~$150-300.
Professional

Professional Massage

Deeper work, expert assessment, full body. Monthly or bi-weekly investment.

  • Types: Swedish, deep tissue, sports massage
  • Frequency: 1-2x/month
  • Best for: Chronic issues, deep knots
  • Duration: 60-90 minutes
  • Cost: $60-120/session
When to go: Persistent pain, pre-competition, after intense training block. Not weekly unless athlete.

Active vs Passive Recovery

When to use each

Active Recovery

Light movement that increases blood flow without adding stress. Promotes recovery through circulation.

  • Walking 20-30 phút
  • Light cycling (Zone 1-2)
  • Swimming easy pace
  • Yoga, stretching
  • Foam rolling session
Use when: DOMS, stiffness, giữa training sessions. Most days nên có some active recovery.

Passive Recovery

Complete rest, no physical stress. Lets body focus 100% on repair. Necessary but don't overdo.

  • Sleep (8-9 hours)
  • Sauna, ice bath
  • Massage
  • Meditation
  • Complete rest day
Use when: After intense blocks, injury, illness, overtraining signs. 1-2x/week max.
💡
Best approach: 80% active recovery, 20% passive. Walking 30 phút > lying on couch. Movement is medicine.

Rest Day Protocol

What to do on rest days

7:00 AM
Wake up + Hydrate
500ml water, light breakfast. No phone first 30 phút.
8:00 AM
Morning Walk (20-30 min)
Light sunlight exposure. Zone 1 cardio. Mental clarity.
10:00 AM
Mobility + Foam Rolling (15 min)
Full body foam roll. Hip, shoulder mobility work.
2:00 PM
Sauna or Contrast Shower (15-20 min)
Heat therapy or hot-cold contrast. Recovery boost.
5:00 PM
Light Activity
Easy bike, swim, or yoga. Keep heart rate low (Zone 1).
9:00 PM
Sleep Preparation
No screens 60 phút. Reading, journaling. Early bed (8-9h sleep).
💡
Key principle: Rest day ≠ lazy day. Active recovery > complete rest. Move your body, just don't stress it.

Bắt đầu recovery protocols

Pick 1-2 protocols từ guide này. Foam rolling daily + sauna 2x/week là great start. Consistency > intensity.
